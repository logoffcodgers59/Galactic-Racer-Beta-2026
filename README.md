## 🕹️ About

STAR WARS: Galactic Racer™ is a high-stakes, runs-based racing adventure from Fuse Games and Secret Mode, set in the lawless Outer Rim after the fall of the Empire. Race across iconic and original Star Wars locations, master repulsorcraft, outmaneuver rivals, and pursue Shade’s story of glory and revenge. The game is scheduled for release on October 6, 2026, and this beta test gives selected players the chance to help shape the experience while receiving a free game key.

Built for Star Wars fans and racing enthusiasts who enjoy skill-driven competition, cinematic worlds, strategic choices, and replayable runs.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🏁 **Runs-Based Racing** — Take on high-stakes races where every run brings new risks, routes, and decisions.
- 🌌 **Outer Rim Circuits** — Race through varied environments across new and iconic Star Wars locations.
- 🚀 **Repulsorcraft Classes** — Choose from multiple vehicle classes with distinct handling and tactical advantages.
- ⚔️ **Rival Takedowns** — Use speed, strategy, sabotage, and brutal takedowns to survive the competition.
- 🎭 **Dynamic Campaign** — Follow Shade’s pursuit of revenge and glory through a branching racing adventure.
- 🏆 **Competitive Multiplayer** — Battle rival pilots for supremacy across fast-paced multiplayer races.
